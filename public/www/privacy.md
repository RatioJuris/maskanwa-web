# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 24, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 24, 2026  

> **Simple Rule:** We do not collect data until you voluntarily participate, nor do we process it alone. Your privacy and autonomy are fundamental to the Maskanwa Open Community.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

* **Voluntary Participation Only:** We strictly adhere to a user-first approach. We do not collect personal data automatically or without your explicit knowledge; information is only gathered when you voluntarily choose to participate, submit details, or update your directory listings.
* **Technical Data:** Standard, non-intrusive server request logs may be processed securely by our infrastructure providers solely to maintain network uptime, security, and routing stability.
* **Cookies:** We utilize minimal, essential cookies required strictly for secure session management.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process information only for the explicit purposes for which you provided it, including:
* Publishing and maintaining verified directory listings for local community entities.
* Responding to direct inquiries, support requests, or update submissions.
* Ensuring platform integrity and complying with applicable administrative or legal requirements.

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Protection

* **No Unilateral Processing or Selling:** We do not sell, trade, or monetize your personal data, nor do we process or share it outside agreed-upon community channels without your knowledge.
* **Security Measures:** We employ industry-standard encryption, secure cloud infrastructure, and strict version-controlled deployment pipelines to safeguard your data against unauthorized access.

---

## 4. Your Rights & Control

You retain full ownership and control over your data. You may request the review, update, or removal of your directory profile at any time through our official community management channels.

---

## 5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or require clarification regarding our data practices, please reach out to us via the official platform administration.
