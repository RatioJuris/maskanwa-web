## 🏛️ About Our Institution

Gayatri Vidhya Mandir Inter College is a premier educational institution serving the student community of Maskanwa and surrounding areas in the Gonda district. We offer a comprehensive curriculum from primary education up to the intermediate level (Class 12), affiliated with the **UP Board**.

### Our Mission
To deliver quality education that balances academic excellence with moral, ethical, and physical development, preparing students to become responsible citizens of India.

### Streams Offered (Class 11 & 12)
* **Science Stream:** Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Biology, Hindi, and English.
* **Arts (Humanities) Stream:** History, Civics, Geography, Economics, Hindi, and English.

## 📝 Student Code of Conduct

To maintain discipline and academic excellence, students are expected to adhere to the following rules:

1. **Punctuality:** Students must arrive at the school premises at least 10 minutes before the morning assembly.
2. **Uniform:** Wearing the prescribed, neat, and tidy school uniform is mandatory every day.
3. **Attendance:** A minimum of 75% attendance is required to be eligible to sit for final board and school examinations.
4. **Behavior:** Respectful behavior toward faculty members, staff, and fellow peers is strictly enforced.
