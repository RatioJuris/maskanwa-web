# Maskanwa Open Community

**Apan Maskanwa, apan pehchaan. ❤️**

**Maskanwa Open Community** is an open digital community for the people, institutions, businesses, and services of the Maskanwa region.

Schools, colleges, shops, professionals, organisations, and local businesses can create their presence on **Maskanwa.com** and become part of the community.

> **You provide the information.  
> The machine takes care of the rest.**

You don't need to know web design, HTML, CSS, or programming.

Just tell people about your school, college, shop, service, or organisation in simple words.

---

## 🌐 What is Maskanwa Open Community?

Think of it as **Maskanwa's open digital address book — but alive.**

A school can have:

`schoolname.maskanwa.com`

A shop can have:

`shopname.maskanwa.com`

A local service can have:

`service.maskanwa.com`

And everyone can discover them through:

**Maskanwa.com**

## ✍️ Tell Your Story

Your website doesn't need complicated design.

Write about your place the way you would normally explain it to another person.

You can include:

- Your name
- What you do
- Where you are located
- Your services
- Courses or products
- Contact information
- Important announcements
- Photographs
- Useful links
- Anything else people should know

**Write naturally.**

The platform takes care of the presentation.
